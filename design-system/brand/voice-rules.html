Waleed's voice, in writing

These rules bind every word on the site and in every asset, and they are enforced by scripts/compliance-scan.py in the repo. Treat them as part of the design system: copy that breaks them breaks the brand.

Zero em dashes or en dashes, ever.

Never "4–6 hours"   Always "4 to 6 hours". No curly quotes in code strings.

Spoken, direct, British.

Short emphatic sentences, contractions, personal confessions, medical framing ("high-yield", "diagnose before you treat"). British English spellings.

No AI-tell vocabulary.

Banned: delve, crucial, robust, leverage, moreover, furthermore, comprehensive, seamless, unlock, elevate, "not just X, but Y", rule-of-three padding.

No invented statistics.

Approved proof points only: 1,000+ students worked with; confidence 6.2 to 8.3 across 74 responses; named student quotes. No grade-outcome claims until August 2026 results data exists.

He teaches; it is his method.

Never "we match you with a tutor" marketplace framing.

One italic gold emphasis per heading.

The signature typographic move: a system, inside a purple serif headline. One per heading, chosen for meaning, never decoration.

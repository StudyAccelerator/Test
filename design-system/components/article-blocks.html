Revision Methods

The Blurting Method: the fastest way to find what you don't know

Dr Waleed Ahmad, MBBS · Updated 16 July 2026 · 9 min read

Quick answer

Blurting means closing your notes and writing everything you can remember about a topic, then checking what you missed. The gaps are your revision list.

Key takeaways

  • Recognition is not recall: re-reading feels productive and moves nothing.
  • One blurt per topic per week; the second comes at least two days later.
Doctor's note

Diagnose before you treat. Rate every topic honestly before a single hour of revision gets scheduled.

How long should a blurt take?

Ten to fifteen minutes with the notes closed, then the same again marking it against the specification.

## What the test proved

1. **Subject-specific "struggling" callouts are the formula.** The two biology-struggling ad sets took 32 of 36 leads at £7.26 to £7.80. "Studying" phrasing of the same shape cost twice as much per lead (£14.43 maths, £19.04 chemistry): the pain word does the work.
2. **Year-group callouts died.** Three Year 12/Year 13 ad sets, zero leads between them.
3. **The doctor-authority and A* angles are support, not headline.** Alone they produced 1 to 2 leads at ~£14.
4. **The buyer demo is mums 45 to 54.** 83 percent of the winner's leads were women, 45 to 54 dominant; campaign-wide, women 26 leads (£10.80 CPL), men 9 (£7.11).
5. **Tracking is sound.** Meta's 36 vs MailerLite's 43 genuine (some organic/late attribution): provenance checking stays.

## Recommendations (proposed 21 August, Waleed decides)

**Long-term always-on campaign:** one campaign, Advantage+ audience, ~£25/day, destination /revision-diagnostic/?for=parents. One ad set carrying six ads: "Is your child struggling with A-level biology / chemistry / maths" each in Doctor-image and plain versions, all in "struggling" phrasing (chemistry and maths get rewritten from "studying"). Expected at test economics: roughly 12 to 20 genuine parent leads a week at £8 to £15 each. £25/day (~£750/month) is the learning budget until the first paid conversion proves the funnel; the scale decision to £50/day comes from lead-to-call-to-sale numbers, not from lead volume.

**Monthly creative test slot** (~£10 to £15/day, 5 to 7 days, one variable at a time):
1. September predictions angle: the predicted-grades card from the 5 Aug ratio set; September is predictions season and pairs with the SEO cluster.
2. Paid-programme callouts (the 2026-08-05 set) pointing at /subject-accelerators/: price angle and scarcity. The two flagged claims (fifth-of-the-cost arithmetic, "5 new students" being real capacity) must be Waleed-verified before launch.
3. First video test: Waleed to camera, 15 to 30 seconds, from the video-script skill; no video has been tested yet.
4. Once real student results exist: a results-proof creative, real numbers only.

**Not recommended:** relaunching the July face creative (worst CPL of everything tested), more year-group callouts, or scaling past £50/day before a sale is attributed.

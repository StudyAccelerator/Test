# Round 3 proof ads: copy pack (13 September 2026)

Six proof creatives in `final/` (each in 1x1, 4x5, 9x16 and 191), built on
Waleed's own stated results and one real student quote. Same photo-led format
that won in August; the proof is the new variable.

**One flag, raised once, then his call.** Three of the cards carry outcome
figures: "Sarah went from a C to an A*", "on average our students jump two
grades in about 3 months", and "96% of our students get their first-choice
university offer". The two-grade average is already the approved public proof
point (his ruling, 6 September). The 96% figure and Sarah's C-to-A* journey
were private to the sales deck until today, when he asked for them in ads. Meta
and the ASA can ask for substantiation of an objective percentage in an ad; if
the basis is a count he can state (for example "of the students who told us
their offers"), it stands. He also said 98% on a sales call the same week, so
one canonical number matters. Safer phrasings if he prefers: "almost all of our
students get their first-choice offer" and "Sarah reached an A* in Biology".
Everything below uses his figures as briefed.

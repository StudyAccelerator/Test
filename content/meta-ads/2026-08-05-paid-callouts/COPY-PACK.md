Everything that goes into Ads Manager alongside the 24 images in `final/`. This set
funnels to the PAID programmes, not the diagnostic. Subject Accelerators sells Biology,
Chemistry and Maths only, so no creative or copy in this pack names Physics.

TWO THINGS ONLY WALEED CAN CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH:
1. The price claim. "A fifth of the cost of 1:1 tutoring" is used on 4 images and in
   Primary Text A. Check the arithmetic against the live programme prices and what UK
   families actually pay for weekly 1:1 tutoring before this runs. If the honest ratio

## Launch checklist

1. NEW campaign ("Paid callouts | Sep 2026"), Leads objective if optimising for the
   Stripe/enquiry flow is not wired, £10 a day to start. Keep it separate from the
   diagnostic campaigns so the funnels never share a budget.
2. Ad set: UK, both parent segments, age suggestion 38 to 55, Facebook only. Broad,
   no interests: the creative does the narrowing, same as the diagnostic test.
3. One ad per image, pairings from the table. Confirm the price ratio and the
   scarcity number FIRST (top of this file).
4. Expectation setting: cold traffic to a paid page converts far below a free quiz,
   so judge this test on cost per landing page view and time to first enquiry, not on
   lead volume in week one. The diagnostic campaign stays the volume engine.

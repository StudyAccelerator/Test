batch but funnelling to the PAID programmes (Subject Accelerators by default). Built to

## Multi-ratio paid set (added 5 August 2026)

`final-ratios/` holds 66 files: 8 messages x their styles x the three Meta ratios
(1080x1080, 1080x1920, 1200x628), generated by `src/gen-ratios.py`.

Messages: the five-students scarcity card (N and S capitalised on Waleed's instruction),
a new "Want a doctor to help your child achieve A*" statement, the predicted-grades
statement (question mark dropped, no highlight, type pushed large), Year 12, Year 13,
and the three subject callouts.

Styles: `marker` (yellow highlight, question mark inside it), `plain` (no highlight,
type enlarged so the words fill the card) and `face` (scrubs photo, circle, TOP LEFT,
placed in normal flow above the text so it can never cover a word). The face variants
keep the highlight on the question cards and stay plain on the two statement cards.

Judgement calls made from dictated instructions, all cheap to reverse:
  - "A-level" kept lowercase-l per site convention; only the N of New and S of Students
    were capitalised as asked.
  - "a doctor" kept rather than "the doctor" on the predicted-grades card.
  - Biology added alongside Maths and Chemistry: Maths appeared twice in the dictated
    list and Biology was missing, and the paid programmes sell all three.

HONESTY FLAG on `02-achieve-astar`: "help your child achieve A*" is the closest this
account has come to a grade claim. It is an offer of help toward the family's own goal
rather than a promise of a result, which is defensible, but it is not the same as
"aiming for A*s" and Waleed should decide knowingly. Until real results data exists
(after 13 August), the safer wording is "Want a doctor to help your child aim for A*s".

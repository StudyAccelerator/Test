**What Locked In never includes, at any price:** 1:1 calls, personal plan reviews, DM coaching, subject teaching. Those are the Study Accelerator (£499), the Mentorship (£2,000 a year) and the Subject Accelerators. The membership teaches and enforces the system at group scale; the programmes go deeper, and the membership is where members discover they want them.

**The delivery commitments, honestly costed:** one hour live on Wednesday evenings in term time, plus the accountability thread inside the daily 20 minutes you already spend. That is the whole marginal cost. If the rota breaks a Wednesday, the session moves or ships as a recorded teach; the promise is weekly rhythm in term time, not a specific unbreakable hour, and the copy below says so plainly.

**Term calendar for the Ward Round (autumn):** weekly from Wednesday 23 September, skipping half term week (w/c 26 October), through to mid December. The November sessions run as the Mock Season Sprint: mock-specific planning, past-paper walkthroughs of method (not subject content), and a mock post-mortem session in December.

## Parent FAQ (for the parent page and for you to answer messages with)

- **Who sees my child's activity?** The community is private to members; all coaching happens in public threads, and Dr Waleed doesn't run private 1:1 chats with members on the platform. That's a deliberate safeguarding decision, not a service gap.
- **What exactly do we pay for?** A weekly live group call in term time, a structured course, and weekly accountability. Not subject tutoring and not 1:1 lessons; those exist separately at alevelaccelerators.com.
- **What if it's not used?** Cancel in two clicks, any month. The accountability check-in exists precisely so an unused subscription becomes visible fast, to you and to us.
- **Why a subscription and not a course?** Because the value is the rhythm. Grades respond to a system run weekly for a term, not to a burst of content watched once.

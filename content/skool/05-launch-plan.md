**The gate on the paid launch, written down so it holds:** Locked In opens on 21 September only if the free community has **at least 60 members and at least 10 non-you members who posted in the last week**. If it is short of that, the date slips two weeks and the fix is more acquisition, not more building. Opening a paid tier inside a quiet room burns the offer permanently; a fortnight's delay burns nothing.

## Your daily routine once live (20 minutes, protect it)

1. Morning (10 min): welcome new joiners by name, answer everything in Ask Waleed, like generously (likes are the points engine that levels people up).
2. Evening (10 min): one comment pass; if it's a scheduled post day, paste the day's post from file 04.
3. That's it. Do not let it become an hour. A founder who burns out in week 3 is worse than one who was 20 minutes a day forever.

## The scoreboard (check weekly, Sunday evening)

Numbers to read straight off Skool's admin metrics, written into a note each Sunday so the trend is real, never remembered:

- Members total, and new this week
- Members active in the last 7 days (Skool shows this)
- Posts and comments this week that are not yours
- Live call attendance
- From 21 Sept: Locked In members, joins, cancels

No targets invented here beyond the paid-launch gate above. First job is honest baselines; October's plan gets written from them.

## What this plan deliberately does not do

- No paid ads for the community. The Meta budget belongs to the diagnostic campaign; the community fills from owned reach. Revisit only after the paid tier proves it converts.
- No website changes yet. Nothing on the site links to Skool until you've approved the community publicly existing; when you do, the natural spots are the Free Tools dropdown and the diagnostic report follow-up email (both drafted in file 06, wired only on your say-so).
- No new content formats. Every post in phase 2 to 3 is written already or pulled from existing material. The build is done; from 10 August the only verbs are send, post, welcome, answer.

# The Skool launch pack

Built 5 August 2026. Everything needed to open, fill and monetise a Skool community under Waleed's own name, written end to end. Nothing in this pack is live; nothing goes live until Waleed approves it.

## The decisions, at a glance

| Decision | Call | Why (full argument in 01-strategy.md) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | **Top 1% A-Levels** | His promise in his own words, and "Top 1%" is already his brand language (workshop, site, 155 subscribers) |
| Architecture | One Skool group, freemium: free plan + paid plan in the same community | Upgrade in place, one room to run |
| Paid tier | **Locked In**, £29 a month founding (first 30, rate locked), then £39. Monthly only | Monthly is the only model that fixes the one-off revenue shape; the June cliff is planned for, not ignored |
| Free launch | Founding email to the student list **Monday 10 August**, results-day wave 13 August | The email is the launch; results week is the year's biggest audience |
| Paid launch | **Monday 21 September**, gated on free-community activity | After the Study Accelerator cohort push, never during it |
| Audience | Students only (16 to 18); parents get a letter and a FAQ, not membership | Candour and safeguarding both demand it |
| Platform plan | Skool Hobby $9 a month, switch to Pro past ~£1,000 a month revenue | Fee arithmetic in 02 |

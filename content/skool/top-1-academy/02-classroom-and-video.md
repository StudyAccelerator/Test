## Course 2: The Workshop Replay (the recording from Saturday 31 October, in four lessons)

Course description:

> The Free Academic Accelerator Workshop, split into its four lessons. If you were there, watch lesson 1 again and do the priority map properly. If you weren't, start here.

Trim the Zoom cloud recording into four clips at the chapter points (cut the first two minutes of hellos and the pitch at the end). Each module: the clip, then this text.

### Lesson 1: How to prioritise and weigh your hours

> Which subject gets your hours this term, and why it's never an equal split. The priority map: one line per subject, confidence out of 10, the gap to your target grade, how much of the final grade this term's topics carry.
>
> **Do this now:** finish your priority map and post a photo of it in Weekly Plans. The subject with the biggest gap and the biggest weight gets the most hours this week.

### Lesson 2: How to crack a topic the first time

> Learn it once so it sticks, instead of relearning the same topic three times before the exam. Close the notes, write what you know, hunt the gaps, fix them, then test again two days later.
>
> **Do this now:** pick the single topic that's worried you most this week and run the method on it tonight. Bring the result to Tuesday's Hot Seat.

### Lesson 3: How to learn topics 80% faster

> The small slice of each subject that carries most of the marks, and how to find it from past papers and the specification. Your high-yield list, five topics per subject, learned properly first.
>
> **Do this now:** write your high-yield list for your weakest subject. Five topics. Everything else waits its turn.

### Lesson 4: Never lose the same mark twice

> Every lost mark gets a line: what the question wanted, what you wrote, the exact gap, and the fix. Then you retest it. The habit that turns every mistake into a mark you keep.
>
> **Do this now:** take your last marked piece of work and log its three biggest lost marks. That's your first upload for Saturday's Marks Clinic.

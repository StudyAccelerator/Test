Written 26 October 2026. Every post below is ready to paste. Dates are the founding cohort's first fortnight; the recurring threads then run every week.

Title: `Start here: welcome to the Top 1% Academy`

>

> 🔥 Wednesday 7pm to 9pm: I teach one method for an hour, then the Hot Seat, where you bring your actual problem and we fix it live. Post your problem in the Hot Seat category before 6pm on Wednesday.

## Launch fortnight

### Sunday 1 November (the day after the workshop, when the first members land)

Category: General. Title: `Who's here? Say hello`

> Founding members are arriving, so let's see who's in. Reply with your year, your subjects, and your biggest worry about this year in one line.
>
> I read every one and I'm building Tuesday's teaching hour around what you post, so the more honest you are, the more Tuesday is about you.

### Monday 2 November

Category: Hot Seat. Title: `Wednesday's Hot Seat is open: drop your problem here`

> First Hot Seat is tomorrow at 8pm, straight after the teaching hour. One post each, one problem, as specific as you can manage.
>
> Good: "I did the priority map and Chemistry comes out top, but my Maths mock is in three weeks. Which wins?"
> Less good: "I'm bad at Maths."
>
> I'll take as many as the hour allows, in the order they land.

### Wednesday 11 November, 5pm

Category: General. Title: `Live in two hours: the Priority Map, properly`

> 7pm on Zoom, link in the Calendar tab. Have your priority map from Saturday in front of you, plus your mock dates if you know them. Cameras off is fine. If you can't make it live, the recording is in the Method Library tonight.

### Wednesday 4 November

Category: Marks Clinic. Title: `Sunday's Marks Clinic: upload your work by Saturday 6pm`

> First Clinic is Saturday at 2pm. Upload a photo or PDF of one piece of real, marked work below: a past paper question you lost marks on, an essay, a plan a teacher commented on. Tell me the subject, the mark you got, and what you think went wrong.
>
> I'll take four to six on screen. Don't tidy it up first. The messy ones teach everyone the most.

### Sunday 15 November, 12pm

Category: General. Title: `Marks Clinic at 2pm: who's on screen`

> List the papers you'll cover (subject and question type, no names) and the live timed section planned for the second hour. Link in the Calendar tab.

### Sunday 8 November

Category: Weekly Plans. Title: `Sunday Reset: how did week one go?`

> Three questions, two minutes each:
> 1. What did you plan versus what did you actually do? No judgement, just data.
> 2. Which topic moved a rating this week? Struggling to Shaky counts. Post it in Wins too.
> 3. What does next week's plan do differently?
>
> Reply below, then post Monday's plan in the morning. This loop, run every week, is the whole system.

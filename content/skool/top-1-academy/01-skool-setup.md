## 0. Account, plan and the billing decision

1. Sign up at skool.com with waleed@alevelaccelerators.com. Both plans carry a 14 day free trial.
2. Pick **Hobby, $9 a month**. Skool's own pricing page lists identical features on both plans (unlimited members, courses, videos, live calls, custom URL, affiliates); the only difference is the fee Skool keeps on payments it processes: 10% on Hobby, 2.9% on Pro ($99 a month). File 04 has the arithmetic.
3. **Bill members through your own Stripe in pounds, not through Skool.** Skool's help centre is explicit: "All subscription prices on Skool are in USD. Members pay in USD." A UK parent would see roughly $133 and $200 a month, moving with the exchange rate, plus their bank's foreign transaction fee. So the Skool group is free to join but **approval-only**, and the Stripe payment link is the door (file 04). Side effect: Skool's 10% fee never applies, so Hobby stays the right plan at any size.
4. At sign-up, read the terms once for a minimum member age (members are 16 to 18). The parent letter in file 02 covers consent either way.

## 7. Rules

Paste into the rules section; they also live in the pinned welcome post (file 03).

> 1. **Real names, real people.** This is a study group, not an anonymous forum.
> 2. **School-corridor standard.** If you wouldn't say it in front of your head of sixth form, don't post it.
> 3. **Ask in public.** Questions go in the feed, not in my DMs. A public answer helps the whole Academy instead of one person, and I don't coach in DMs, full stop.
> 4. **What's shared here stays here.** People post real marks and real mistakes in the Hot Seat and the Clinic. Screenshots leaving the group means leaving the group.
> 5. **No selling, no links to other groups, no answer-begging for homework.** Instant removal.
> 6. **Struggling with more than studying?** Talk to someone properly: Samaritans 116 123, Childline 0800 1111, or your GP. We care about you here, and they can actually help.

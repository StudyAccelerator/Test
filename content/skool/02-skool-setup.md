## 7. Rules (paste into the rules/guidelines section, and they live in the pinned welcome post too)

> 1. **Real names, real people.** This is a study community, not an anonymous forum.
> 2. **School-corridor standard.** If you wouldn't say it in front of your head of sixth form, don't post it.
> 3. **Ask in public.** Post questions in the feed, not in my DMs. Public answers help 200 people instead of one. I don't do 1:1 coaching in DMs, full stop.
> 4. **No selling anything, no links to your Discord, no homework-answer begging.** Instant removal.
> 5. **Struggling with more than revision?** If things are heavy, talk to someone properly: Samaritans 116 123, Childline 0800 1111, or your GP. This community cares about you, and those people can actually help.

## 8. Moderation posture (for you, not for pasting)

- Check the feed morning and evening; the daily routine in file 05 is 20 minutes total.
- Remove rule-breakers fast and quietly. One warning, then out.
- If a member posts anything that reads as a disclosure (self-harm, abuse, crisis): reply publicly with warmth and the rule 5 signposts, take the detail out of the public feed, and keep a dated note of what you saw and did. You know this drill from the day job; the note-keeping matters here too.
- DMs from members get one-line redirects to the feed. No exceptions, even nice ones. The rule protects you as much as them.

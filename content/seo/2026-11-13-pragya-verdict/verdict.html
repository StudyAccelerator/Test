3. Why her approach cannot reach your goal

  1. The buyer search is a marketplace fight. Parents typing "A-level tutor" get The Profs, MyTutor and Tutorful, established sites with years of links and reviews behind them. Bulk profile and bookmarking links will never close that gap.
  2. Google is built to ignore this kind of link. Self-made blogs, nofollow posts and noindexed pages are the textbook low-value link. At best they do nothing. Copied articles can work against you.
  3. The brief was changed three times and the output stayed the same. On 24 September you set the condition that real UK education and parenting placements had to start landing. On 2 October target-finding moved in house to make that easy. Six weeks later there are none.

4. What to do next, in order

  1. End the contract this week. Send the reply below, then end the contract in Upwork yourself. Ask her for the list of accounts made in your name, and for the copied website articles to come down.
  2. Read Search Console once, from your Mac. Start a session in the Claude desktop app and say "run the SEO optimiser". It takes about five minutes and records where every page stands now, so you can see that nothing drops after she stops.
  3. Put the £70 a week into a buyer test. Run Google Ads on tutor searches at £10 a day for three weeks. The spec is already written (content/google-ads/2026-10-02-search-test.md). It answers the real question directly: do parents searching for a tutor book calls with you? If they do, organic work on those terms is worth chasing. If they don't, you've saved months.
  4. Keep the in-house content engine, pointed at parents. The subject tutoring pages and parent articles should go after "A-level tutor for my child" searches, with every one leading to the diagnostic and the free call.
  5. Get the few links that matter yourself. One article on The Parents' Guide To or Families Online is worth more than all 118 of these. The outreach emails are already drafted (content/seo/2026-08-09-listicle-outreach-drafts.md).
  6. Fix the lead tracking. Record a visitor's source on the first page they land on, so a Google reader who goes from a blog post to the diagnostic is credited to Google. This is a small change that is ready to build on your go.

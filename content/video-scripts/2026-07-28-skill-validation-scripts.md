## Script C: September, and the Subject Accelerator cohort

**Idea as received:** "the September Subject Accelerator cohort starts 6 September"
**Classification:** students (new Year 13 mainly), funnel target = Subject Accelerators, timed for late August into early September.
**Format:** Quote Unpacking (the batch-1 format 3 skeleton). **Hook family:** contrarian ("X is NOT Y" family, the pattern proven by Mr Salles at 23x his account baseline).
**Facts used:** your established confession that your own method was terrible at first (brand-central, in your own workshop words); September cohort start date of 6 September per your brief of 28 July. No results claims, no prices.
**Wardrobe:** scrubs.

**[Beat: an interesting sentence where the meaning is not obvious. Intrigue first.]**

> Your A-level grade is decided in September. Not in May. Not in the exam hall. September.

*Pause. Look at the camera.*

> Let me explain.

*On-screen text, first frame: "your grade is decided in September"*

**[Beat: unpack the statement, keeping loops open, revealing slowly.]**

> Because here's what actually happens. Everyone works hard in April. Everyone. By Easter the whole year group is doing past papers, and the gaps between students barely move. So if everyone's working hard at the end, why do the same people keep winning?

> It's the system they built at the start. When I was in year 13, I started September with a method that was genuinely terrible. Rereading, highlighting, beautiful notes. I looked busy for months before I realised none of it scores marks.

**[Beat: the explanation, landing the quote with its real meaning.]**

> The students who get the As in May are testing themselves from week one. Blank page, notes shut, before it feels necessary. Content's still light in September, so the habit costs you nothing. By spring it's automatic. That's what the sentence means. Your grade is decided in September. Decide it.

> And if you'd rather not build the system alone, that's literally what I do. The September cohort starts on the sixth. Link's in my bio.

*On-screen text at the end: the quote again, underneath: "September = the system. May = the score."*

**Caption:** The most useful thing I can tell a new Year 13. September decides May.
**Hashtags:** #alevels #year13 #alevelrevision #backtoschool #studytok

**Word count and runtime:** 178 spoken words, so 52 to 59 seconds. Top of the quote-unpacking window: aim for the quicker read, it is written to move. If it still drifts past 55 on the timed read, the backup cut is "Because here's what actually happens."

**Why this should hold:** the quote is genuinely ambiguous for the first few seconds, the "why do the same people keep winning?" loop only closes at the reveal, and the quote re-landing with new meaning at the end is the rewatch trigger, exactly the engine of the batch-1 quote-unpacking script. The confession beat keeps it warm instead of preachy, and the cohort line is one sentence, honest, after the value has already been given.

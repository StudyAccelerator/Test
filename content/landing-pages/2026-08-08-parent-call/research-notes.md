# Competitor teardown: EdViro Academy and Victor Alvarez (8 August 2026)

Research behind the parent call-funnel landing page in this folder. Every claim below was read directly from the named source on 8 August 2026. Where something is an inference, it says so. No performance figure in here is verified revenue; see "what the records actually show" for both.

## 1. EdViro Academy (the webinar machine)

**Who they are.** Facebook page "Edviro Academy : GCSE Maths Science & English Specialist" (2.2K followers, zero page reviews). Founder fronts everything: Vishnudas, a mechanical engineer and engineering university lecturer. Their site (edviroacademy.co.uk) sells live group GCSE and A-level lessons, seven named teachers, no public prices. GCSE-only in the ads.

**The ad machine (Meta Ads Library, 8 Aug).** About 20 active ads. The main batch has run unchanged since 5 June 2026, with refreshes on 12, 25 and 26 June: nine-plus weeks of continuous spend on the same creatives. Every single ad drives the same destination: a free live Zoom "Masterclass" at webinar.edviroacademy.co.uk. Videos around one minute, titled "How to get a GCSE Grade 9 in 3 steps".

**The ad copy template** (one master long-form structure, many variants):
1. Callout by year group and ambition: "Parents of Year 9, 10 & 11, if your child is aiming for Grade 8s and 9s, read this."
2. Founder credential intro in first person.
3. Shock stats: over half of GCSE students below Grade 5, only about 4 percent hit Grade 9, "The gap isn't ability. It's method."
4. Problem list with cross marks (wrong revision, no examiner awareness, time management, wordy questions).
5. Mechanism reframe: "GCSE exams are designed as a filter."
6. Tick-list of what the masterclass teaches.
7. Aspiration and urgency close (sixth form, universities, medicine, law; "Not after mocks. Now.")
8. CTA to save a seat, personal sign-off.
Variants include teacher-voiced versions where staff vouch for the founder (Ash; Lakhvir, "19 years, former Head of Chemistry at London grammar schools").

**The landing page** (webinar-registration): "Attention! Parents of GCSE Years 9, 10 & 11 Students" banner, headline "The 3-Step Method to Help Your Child Jump At Least 2 Grades in GCSE Maths, Science & English", subhead claiming "trusted by 1,500+ UK families, with 92% of students improving by at least 2 grades", urgency line "Usually fills 24hrs before we go live", form capturing name, email, required phone and year group, first-person CTA "Yes Vish, I Want to Attend!", benefit bullets, four grade-jump testimonials, founder bio. No legal footer, no privacy link.

**What the records actually show.** No Companies House entity matching "Edviro" exists (searched 8 Aug; the only hits are unrelated Edviron plant-hire companies). An officer search for Vishnudas is inconclusive. The Trustpilot result that comes up in search belongs to edvi.app, a different business. The 92 percent / 1,500 families / 2,000 students figures are self-published and inconsistent between their own ads (2,000+ students and 4 years in one ad, 1,000+ and 9 years in another, "3 years" in a third; Grade 9 rate quoted as 3, 4 and 5 percent in different ads). One ad runs a literal "guarantee... at least 2 grades" line. **The only hard evidence of performance is behavioural: roughly 20 creatives kept live for nine-plus weeks with a weekly live webinar cadence. An operation only keeps paying for that if the economics at least cover themselves. That is an inference, not a record.**

## 2. Victor Alvarez / GCSEALevelStudent (the call funnel)

**Who he is.** Facebook page "Victor Alvarez - GCSE, A-Level & IB Tutoring", 1K followers. A London (Sidcup) based Spanish and French tutor of 10 years, founder of "The A* Student", expanding into Maths, English and Science. Organic reach is tiny: his latest visible page post (10 April) has 1 reaction and 5 comments, and he runs "Comment START" mechanics.

**The ads (Meta Ads Library, 8 Aug).** About 6 active ads, running since 1 to 3 June 2026. This is the direct-to-call funnel: no webinar. Structure: question callout ("Are they getting ready for the GCSE Exams?"), scarcity ("I'm looking for 10 students who want to achieve a top-grade"), cross-mark struggle list, "Then I can help!", founder intro plus "3-step system" mechanism, "Why should you listen to me?" proof block with named grade-jump case studies ("Lisa Jacob went from a 5 in October to 8 in December", "Conor Hay... A* ... more than $90,000 in scholarships"), then the close: "All you have to do is click the link, enter your details, and schedule a time to speak with me." Student counts vary between ads (400, 500, 1000+) and two live ads still say "exams in 2025".

**The landing page** (the optin URL): a bought funnel template, visibly half-finished. Lorem ipsum in the bio, a literal "[TEACHER NAME]" placeholder, a broken scarcity counter reading "ONLY 15 5 Spaces Left", testimonial images that don't load. The mechanics underneath are the interesting part: an all-caps headline promising a jump of 1 to 3 grades, for children in Year 10 to 13, in under 6 months; the subhead "WITHOUT WASTING TIME USING BOOKS OR LONG STUDY GUIDES!"; phone-number-first capture ("Without a VALID number we will be unable to send you ANY INFORMATION"), callback promise ("We will be giving you a call in the next 24 hours"), CTA "Send Me The Next Steps", and a liability disclaimer footer.

**What the records actually show.** The template vendor whose copyright sits in his footer, Rockstar Online Tutor Ltd (13115761), was dissolved on 12 May 2026. No Companies House entity is visible for Victor's own operation in a name search; most likely a sole trader. No independent review presence found. **Verdict: a small solo operation running the right funnel shape with sloppy execution. The mechanic is instructive; the execution is a warning.**

## 3. What both prove for us

1. **Founder-fronted parent ads are the working pattern in this market.** Both operations put a named person with a non-teaching credential (engineer; 10-year tutor) at the front. Nobody runs faceless brand ads. This confirms the front-of-business decision (see content/business-audit/2026-08-06-front-of-business-decision.md).
2. **Both monetise through a human step**, never a cold checkout: Edviro through a live webinar, Victor through a booked call. This is the appointment layer the 17 July lead-generation map said our funnel was missing.
3. **"The gap isn't ability, it's method" is being paid for in-market, right now**, in those words. Our brand line ("Top grades are a system, not a talent") is the same claim with a better messenger. We are not inventing a demand; we are entering one with a stronger credential.
4. **Longevity of spend is the only public performance signal either has.** Neither files revenue anywhere. Anyone claiming to know their sales is guessing.

## 4. What the page in this folder borrows, and what it refuses

**Borrowed:** the parent callout with year-group specificity; first-person founder copy; the struggle checklist in the parent's own words; the method-not-ability reframe; a single human next step (the call); testimonials next to the promise; the qualification block; urgency framing; the PS; the sticky mobile CTA; long-form single-purpose page with one repeated CTA.

**Refused, deliberately:** grade-jump promises and percentage claims (we have no results data until August 2026, the content rules ban invented numbers, and the ASA would eat the "guarantee 2 grades" line alive); fake or broken scarcity counters; inconsistent proof numbers; the required-phone-with-warnings capture (hostile to a calm doctor brand); the webinar step (the ask was a call funnel; the recorded parents' masterclass is a separate play in the lead-generation map); a "we'll call you in 24 hours" promise we cannot staff around an NHS rota. Our scarcity is real (his rota) and stated as such.

**Framework mapping.** Hormozi: audience-plus-problem headline, the three-second test answered above the fold, one CTA repeated four times, proof density near claims, risk reversal ("the honest worst case" plus the PS), scarcity only where true. Value equation: likelihood up via doctor plus measured proof; time delay down via "what you leave the call with"; effort down via "nothing to prepare". Suby (Sell Like Crazy): enter the conversation in the parent's head (the checklist section), give the diagnosis before any pitch (magic lantern), a Godfather-shaped offer (free, no obligation, keep the plan either way, nobody chased), qualification to protect show rate, the PS.

# Top 1% Mentorship: the curriculum

Built 10 September 2026 for the first two mentorship students (one Year 12, one resitting), starting the week of 14 September. Weekly 60-minute one-to-one on Zoom, £300 a month, method only, never subject content. The scoreboard is timed papers plus the tracker's topic ratings; parents get a written update at the end of every month.

| File | What it is | When to open it |
|---|---|---|
| [01-the-spine.md](01-the-spine.md) | The principles, the four tiers mapped to six months, the six phases, the two tracks, the scoreboard, the session shape, the retention design | Once, before session 1, and whenever you are about to deviate |
| [02-weekly-runsheets.md](02-weekly-runsheets.md) | Sessions 1 to 26 (14 September to 28 March), one page each: why this week, the number, the sixty minutes, what they walk away with, the between-session work, deviate if, the Year 12 and Resit notes. Then the run-in to the exams, sketched | Every week, at the week |
| [03-templates.md](03-templates.md) | The scorecard, session notes, the welcome messages, the monthly parent update, the missed-session message, the contact rules | Before session 1, then whenever a template is due |
| [04-sustainability.md](04-sustainability.md) | What breaks first, what to change with two students, what the offer becomes at each size, what to give up, and the lines on the live site that do not match | Now, and again at student six |

The whole pack renders to one PDF with `python3 content/mentorship/render.py` (headless Chromium, output `content/mentorship/Top-1-Mentorship-Curriculum.pdf`, gitignored: rebuild it after edits). The spine and the runsheets are on the HQ dashboard's Playbooks panel.

## Standing rules for this folder

- The spine order (retrieval, spacing, targeting, exam craft) does not change. The pace does, per student, and every deviation is written in the session notes.
- No week without a number that should move. If a session cannot name one, it is not in the plan.
- Never subject content. Sessions that drift into "explain this topic" refer to the teacher, the textbook or a Subject Accelerator.
- The run-in (sessions 27 to 36) is written in full in February 2027, after two real students have been through phases one to four. Do not write it earlier from theory.
- When Waleed changes the offer (cadence, the number, the cap, parent updates), update file 04's site table and CLAUDE.md the same day.

Hi {$name},

Schools are oddly bad at telling parents the shape of the year in advance, so everything arrives as a surprise. Here's the map, the five moments that actually matter, and what each one needs from your household.

**Autumn term: the evidence window.** Teachers set and review predicted grades off what they see this term: early tests, classwork, mocks. For Year 13, this is the term that writes the UCAS form. What it needs: the method working NOW, not after Christmas. This is why I bang the September drum so hard, and it's the single most useful thing on this whole map.

**Mid-October (Year 13, some subjects): the early UCAS deadline.** Medicine, dentistry, veterinary and Oxbridge applications go in around mid-October, everything else by the main January deadline. What it needs: if your child is anywhere near those courses, the personal statement conversation should be happening already, not in half term week.

**November to December: mocks, round one.** The first full-dress rehearsal, and the most common ambush of the year, because content from a whole year ago reappears. What it needs: revision that started weeks out, and, more importantly, a calm household response to whatever the results are. A rough November mock, read properly, is a gift with six months left on the clock.

**January to March: the long grind.** No big events, which is exactly the danger. This is where methods quietly collapse and nobody notices until spring. What it needs: the weekly rhythm holding, and someone occasionally checking the plan is still on the wall, not the drama of a crisis, just maintenance.

**April to June: exam season.** By now the work is mostly done or it isn't, and the marginal gains move to sleep, routine and exam technique. What it needs from you: food, calm and lifts. Truly, by this point, the parenting job is logistics and steadiness.

The pattern worth noticing: every one of these moments goes better if the fix happened in the autumn, and gets harder to influence as the year runs on. That's not a sales line, it's just the calendar, and now you've seen the whole of it at once.

Stick this email somewhere you can find it in November.

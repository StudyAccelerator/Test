{$name},

This is the last email in this series, so let me close it the way I'd close a consultation.

Two weeks ago you downloaded a guide. Since then you've had the questions that replace guessing with evidence, the process swap for "have you revised", the science of why revision fades, the honest read on tutoring, the three paths compared, and the timing truth about the autumn term. Whatever you do next, you're doing it informed, and informed parents make better calls than worried ones. That was the whole point.

From here, I stop the regular emails. No drip, no countdowns. If something genuinely matters to your child's year, I'll write once, plainly, and that's it.

One small ask before I go quiet. Reply and tell me where you landed, even in five words. "Running the free plan." "Booked the call." "Went with a tutor." "Still watching." I read every reply, I don't argue with anyone's decision, and each one makes this better for the next family. If the honest answer is "still confused", say that, because untangling it usually takes one question.

Everything stays where it was: the guide, the free diagnostic, the tracker, the half hour call, the programmes. Doors don't expire around here.

And keep asking the process questions. They're free, they take ten seconds, and they'll tell you more about how this year is really going than any report card.

Best wishes,

Waleed

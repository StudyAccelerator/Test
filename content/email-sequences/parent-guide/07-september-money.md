**Predicted grades are set in the autumn**

Predicted grades go on the UCAS form. They shape which universities offer your child a place.

They get set and checked in the autumn term. And they're based on what teachers see: early Year 13 tests, mocks, classwork.

So the next twelve or so weeks aren't just one more term. This is the term where your child's paper record gets set.

**Why now is worth more than January**

Predicted grades follow the evidence. Autumn is when that evidence gets made.

Fix the method or the exam skills now. Teachers then see three months of better work.

The same fix made in January still helps the real exams. But by then those grades are set. Some doors that were open in October have closed.

Same fix, very different value, just because of the date.

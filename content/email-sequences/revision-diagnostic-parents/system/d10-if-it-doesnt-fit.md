**"What if it turns out not to be right for {$diag_child_name|default('my child')}?"** The first session is risk-free: if it isn't valuable, you can ask for a full refund, no questions asked. It's on the page in writing. One real session tells you more than another two weeks of weighing it up from the outside.

**"What if they resist the whole idea?"** Then don't buy it, genuinely. A programme bought for a student who hasn't agreed to it is wasted money, whatever the programme. Start free instead: the one question, the report's plan, the tracker. If two weeks of the free version moves anything at all, the conversation about help changes on its own, and the levels will still be there.

**"What if they start strong and fade by week three?"** Week three is exactly why the Accelerator level exists. The fade isn't a character flaw. It's what happens to every system that runs on willpower alone, and mine did exactly the same at that age. The weekly accountability is the part of the product that catches it, so the fade risk is actually an argument for that level, not against the whole idea.

**"What if we pick the wrong level?"** Then we move you. Series to Accelerator if the follow-through needs holding, or an honest conversation about whether the free plan is doing enough. Nobody gets left on the wrong option out of politeness.

**"What if it turns out not to be right for {$diag_child_name|default('my child')}?"** The first session is risk-free: if it isn't valuable, you can request a full refund, no questions asked. It's on the page in writing. One real session of evidence beats another fortnight of weighing it up from the outside.

**"What if they resist the whole idea?"** Then don't buy it, genuinely. A programme bought for a student who hasn't agreed to it is burning money, whatever the programme. Start free instead: the one question, the report's plan, the tracker. If a fortnight of the free version moves anything at all, the conversation about help changes on its own, and the levels will still be there.

**"What if they start strong and fade by week three?"** Week three is precisely why the Accelerator level exists. The fade isn't a character flaw, it's what happens to every system that runs on willpower alone, mine included at that age. Weekly accountability is the part of the product that catches it, which is why the fade risk is an argument for that level rather than against the whole idea.

**"What if we pick the wrong level?"** Then we move you. Series to Accelerator if the follow-through needs holding, or the honest conversation about whether the free plan is doing enough. Nobody gets stranded on a wrong choice out of politeness.

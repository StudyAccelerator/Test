Hi {$name},

Four questions I hear about the Study System, answered straight.

**"Shouldn't they just discipline themselves?"** Here's what I've seen across more than a thousand students: the ones who revise consistently don't have more discipline, they have fewer decisions to make. The session is already decided, the topic is already decided, the method is already decided. That's what a system means. Expecting a seventeen year old to invent all that alone, in the middle of Year 13, under pressure, is how most households end up stuck in the nagging loop.

**"What evidence is there?"** The method itself, testing yourself and returning to material before it fades, is the most repeated finding in the whole science of learning. On average, our students jump two grades. And I've spent six years and over 1,000 students getting teenagers to actually run it, which is the part that makes the difference.

**"Why not just get a tutor?"** A tutor improves one subject while the underlying method keeps failing in all of them. Fix the method and every subject improves, including the ones you were never going to pay for tutoring in. And if there already is a tutor, the system makes those expensive hours finally stick.

**"What if they resist the whole idea?"** Then start free and small: the one question from my first email, plus the tracker. If they run the free week and feel the difference, the conversation changes on its own, and the levels will still be there.

Here are four questions I hear from parents about the Study System, answered straight.

**"Shouldn't they just discipline themselves?"**

Here's what I've seen across more than a thousand students. The ones who revise each week don't have more discipline. They have fewer choices to make.

The session is set in advance, the topic is set, and the method is set. That's what a system means.

Asking a seventeen year old to invent all of that alone, in the middle of Year 13, under pressure, is how most homes end up with nagging each night.

**"What proof is there?"**

The method itself, testing yourself and going back to a topic before it fades, is the most repeated finding in the whole science of learning.

On average, our students jump two grades. And I've spent six years and over 1,000 students getting teenagers to run it for real, which is the part that makes it work.

**"Why not just get a tutor?"**

A tutor lifts one subject while the method under it keeps failing in all of them. Fix the method and each subject improves, even the ones you'd never pay for tutoring in.

And if there's a tutor in place, the system makes those costly hours stick at last.

**"What if they push back on the whole idea?"**

Then start free and small: the one question from my first email, plus the tracker. If they run the free week and feel the change, the talk changes on its own. The levels will still be there.

By this point you know what the Subject Accelerator is, what it costs and what evidence sits behind it. What's usually left is quieter: the what-ifs. Here are the real ones, with the honest answers.

**"What if it turns out not to be right for {$diag_child_name|default('my child')}?"** The first session is risk-free. If it isn't valuable, you can request a full refund, no questions asked. That's on the programme page in writing, and it exists precisely so this decision doesn't have to be perfect in advance. One session of evidence beats a fortnight of deliberating.

**"What if they miss a week?"** Every session is recorded. Weekends go wrong, families have lives, and one missed Sunday shouldn't unravel a term.

**"What if the group is too big for them to matter in it?"** Groups are capped small on purpose, because the sessions are built around students' own answers being worked on against the mark scheme. A session where your child's answers are never looked at would be a session that isn't doing its job.

**"What if they start and won't engage?"** Then we should talk, early, not at week six. I'd rather move a student onto the right thing, including off the programme, than have them sit politely in the wrong one. You'd have a doctor's honest read on it, not a retention script.

By this point you know what the Subject Accelerator is, what it costs and what evidence sits behind it. What's usually left are the quieter worries. Here are the real ones, with honest answers.

**"What if it turns out not to be right for {$diag_child_name|default('my child')}?"** The first session is risk-free. If it isn't valuable, you can ask for a full refund, no questions asked. That's on the programme page in writing, and it exists so you don't have to get the decision perfectly right in advance. One real session tells you more than another two weeks of weighing it up.

**"What if they miss a week?"** Every session is recorded. Weekends go wrong, families have lives, and one missed Sunday shouldn't undo a term.

**"What if the group is too big for them to matter in it?"** Groups are capped small on purpose, because the sessions are built around students' own answers being worked on against the mark scheme. A session where your child's answers never get looked at wouldn't be doing its job.

**"What if they start and won't engage?"** Then we should talk, early, not at week six. I'd much rather move a student onto the right thing, including off the programme, than have them sit politely in the wrong one. You'd get a doctor's honest read, not a script designed to keep you paying.

Hi {$name},

By now you know what the Subject Accelerator is, what it costs and what sits behind it. What's often left are the quieter worries, so here are the real ones, answered.

**"What if it turns out not to be right for {$diag_child_name|default('my child')}?"**

That's what the satisfaction guarantee is for. Join the first session, and if you're not completely satisfied, you get your money back, no questions asked.

It's on the programme page in writing. It exists so you don't have to get the decision right in advance. One real session tells you more than two more weeks of thinking it over.

**"What if they miss a week?"**

Each session is recorded. Weekends go wrong, families have lives, and one missed Sunday shouldn't undo a term.

**"What if the group is too big for them to matter in it?"**

Groups are kept small on purpose, because the sessions are built around students' own answers being worked on against the mark scheme. Your child's answers get looked at in each session.

**"What if they start and struggle to engage?"**

Then we talk, early, not at week six. I stay close to the students in each cohort. I'd much rather move a student onto the right thing than have them sit quietly in the wrong one.

If there's a what-if I've missed, that's what the free academic strategy call is for. Bring the worry, and you'll leave with a straight answer and a clear plan for {$diag_child_name|default('your child')}. I'm confident we can help.

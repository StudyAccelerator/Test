Hi {$name},

Here are the four questions parents ask me most about the Subject Accelerator, answered the way I answer them on calls.

**"Can a group fix MY child's wording?"**

Fair question. Groups are kept small on purpose. The sessions are built around students' own answers being reworked against the mark scheme live, theirs included.

And here's the part no one expects. Watching someone else's answer get fixed teaches almost as much as your own, because students make very similar mistakes. It's often the same missing "because" or the same vague verb.

Within two sessions they start hearing their own mistakes in other people's answers, and that's when it starts to stick.

**"What evidence do you have?"**

On average, our students jump two grades. The specialists who teach got top grades in the subject themselves. They have helped hundreds of students do the same. I've worked with over 1,000 A-level students across six years.

Naysa, biology, put it like this: "The exam question walk-throughs were brilliant, really focused on applying what we know rather than just repeating content."

**"Will it clash with school or sport?"**

It's built not to. The sessions are weekend slots only, the subjects never clash, and each session is recorded in case one gets missed.

Between sessions, we ask for two to three hours of work on their own. That's mostly past paper practice they should be doing in any case, now with coaching behind it.

**"Is £339 worth it?"**

It costs less than seven hours of 1:1 tutoring at the usual £50 an hour. That buys twelve weeks of coaching in the one skill the diagnostic flagged. And the first session is covered by our satisfaction guarantee, so your money is safe.

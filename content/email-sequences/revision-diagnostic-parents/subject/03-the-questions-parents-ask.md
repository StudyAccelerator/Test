Four questions, answered the way I answer them on calls.

**"Can a group fix MY child's wording?"** Fair question. Groups are kept small on purpose, and the sessions are built around students' own answers being reworked against the mark scheme live, theirs included. And here's the part nobody expects: watching someone else's answer get fixed teaches almost as much as your own, because students make surprisingly similar mistakes. The same missing "because", the same vague verb. Within two sessions they start hearing their own mistakes in other people's answers, and that's when it starts to stick.

**"What evidence do you have?"** On average, our students jump two grades. The specialists who teach got top grades in the subject themselves and have helped hundreds of students do the same, and I've worked with over 1,000 A-level students across six years. In students' own words, Naysa, biology: "The exam question walk-throughs were brilliant, really focused on applying what we know rather than just repeating content."

**"Will it clash with school or sport?"** It's built not to. Weekend slots only, the subjects never overlap, and every session is recorded if one gets missed. Between sessions the expectation is two to three hours of independent work, which is mostly past paper practice they should be doing anyway, now with coaching behind it.

**"Is £339 worth it?"** It's under seven hours of average 1:1 tutoring money, spread across twelve weeks of specialist coaching in the one skill the diagnostic flagged. And the first session is covered by our satisfaction guarantee, so if you're not completely satisfied, you get your money back.

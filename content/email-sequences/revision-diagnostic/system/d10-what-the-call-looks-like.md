A few students have replied asking some version of the same thing: "what actually happens if I book the call?" Which is a polite way of asking "is this where the sales pitch lives?" Fair. Here's the agenda, so you can decide with the lights on.

**Minutes 0 to 5:** you talk, I listen. Your subjects, your year, what the diagnostic said, and what your week actually looks like: school, work, sport, the lot. A system that ignores your real week isn't a system, it's a wish.

**Minutes 5 to 20:** we look at how the method is landing. Which parts you've run, which parts wobbled, where the returns are getting skipped. I tell you what I'd fix first and, just as usefully, what to stop worrying about. Most students are trying to fix four things at once, which is why none of them move.

**Minutes 20 to 30:** you leave with a two week plan, written down. Which topics, which sessions, where the returns go. Specific enough that Sunday-you knows exactly what Monday-you is doing.

That's the whole call. If the Study System genuinely fits how your fortnight has gone, I'll say so in one sentence and you can ask about it or not. If the honest answer is "you're running it fine alone, keep your money", that's what you'll hear. I'm a doctor before I'm a business owner, and I don't prescribe things people don't need.

**Who shouldn't book:** if you haven't tried a single blurt yet and know you won't before the call, spend the 30 minutes on that instead. The call works best with even one week of real attempts in it.

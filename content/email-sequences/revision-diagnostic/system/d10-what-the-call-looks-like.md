A few students have replied asking the same thing in different words: "what actually happens if I book the call?" Which is a polite way of asking whether this is where the sales pitch lives. Fair question. So here's exactly what happens, and you can decide for yourself.

**First five minutes:** you talk, I listen. Your subjects, your year, what the diagnostic said, and what your week actually looks like: school, work, sport, all of it. A system that ignores your real week isn't a system, it's wishful thinking.

**The middle twenty:** we look at how the method is going. Which parts you've run, which parts wobbled, where the returns got skipped. I tell you what I'd fix first, and just as usefully, what to stop worrying about. Most students are trying to fix four things at once, and that's exactly why none of them move.

**The last five:** you leave with a plan for the next two weeks, written down. Which topics, which sessions, where the returns go. Specific enough that when Monday comes, you already know what you're doing.

That's the whole call. If the Study System genuinely fits how your fortnight has gone, I'll say so in one sentence, and you can ask about it or not. And if the honest answer is "you're running it fine on your own, keep your money", that's exactly what you'll hear. I'm a doctor before I'm a business owner, and I don't prescribe things people don't need.

**Who shouldn't book:** if you haven't tried a single blurt yet and you know you won't before the call, spend the 30 minutes on that instead. The call works best with even one week of real attempts behind it.

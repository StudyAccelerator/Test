**"I've tried flashcards and Anki before. It didn't stick."**

Right, and here's why. A flashcard is a tool, and you were handed the tool without the system around it. Anki without scheduled sessions just becomes an app that makes you feel guilty. That structure is exactly what the Study System sets up, so the tools you already own finally have somewhere to live.

**"My subjects are essay subjects. Does this even apply?"**

Completely. Retrieval isn't just a science thing. Blurting an essay plan from memory, the arguments, the evidence, the quotes, is the single best essay preparation I know of. And the error log works even better for essays than for calculations, because examiners point out the same problems again and again. The system works for every subject, and that's the whole argument for fixing the method rather than paying someone to patch one subject.

**"I don't have time for a system on top of everything."**

The system isn't on top of your work. It replaces the expensive habits with cheaper ones. Students on this system usually end up studying fewer hours, not more, and that's the point.

**"I've tried flashcards and Anki before. It didn't stick."**

**"My subjects are essay subjects. Does this even apply?"**

Yes, fully. Pulling things out of your head isn't just a science thing.

Blurting an essay plan from memory, the points, the evidence, the quotes, is the best essay prep I know of.

And the error log works even better for essays than for sums, because markers point out the same problems again and again.

The system works for each subject. That's the whole case for fixing the method rather than paying someone to patch one subject.

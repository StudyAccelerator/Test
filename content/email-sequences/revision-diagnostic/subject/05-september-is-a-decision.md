**The bridge plan for {$diag_worry_subject|default('your priority subject')}**

Between now and the cohort, here's the plan, in six lines:

Each week, one timed section (the drill from my first email), marked harshly, letters logged. Watch how your K, R, W and T counts change. That's your progress tracker now.

Every K goes on a content list. Fix those with blurting: blank page, book closed, gaps in a second colour, and come back to the topic on day 2 and day 5.

Two minutes at the start of each session: the examiner's dictionary, out loud.

Every W rewritten in the scheme's language, by hand, said out loud.

Sundays: retest your error log. Twenty minutes. It's the cheapest way to win marks back that I know of.

And rest properly. Arriving in September exhausted defeats the whole point.

Do that and you arrive at the start of term already moving, whether or not you ever join anything of mine.

**The September decision**

Here's the honest version of why timing matters, with no countdown clock attached.

The cohort starts Sunday 13th September and runs twelve weeks, which covers exactly the period when autumn assessments happen and predicted grades get set. Join in September and your coached answers become the evidence your teachers see. That's not a sales trick, it's just the school calendar, and it's the same calendar whether I mention it or not.

Places are capped because the sessions only work in small groups, so when a subject fills, it fills. If you already know you're in, booking now just takes the decision off your plate and lets you spend the in-between weeks on the bridge plan. And if you're genuinely unsure, don't buy on urgency, mine or anyone else's. Run two more weeks of the drill, look at your letters, and let the evidence decide. A log full of W and T says this programme. A log full of K says fix content first, and I'll tell you the same thing on a call for free.

**The bridge plan for {$diag_worry_subject|default('your priority subject')}**

Between now and the cohort, here's the plan, in six lines.

Each week, one timed section (the drill from my first email), marked harshly, letters logged. Watch how your K, R, W and T counts change, because that's your progress tracker now.

Every K goes on a content list. Fix those with blurting: blank page, book closed, gaps in a second colour, and come back to the topic on day 2 and day 5.

Two minutes at the start of each session: the examiner's dictionary, out loud.

Every W rewritten in the scheme's language, by hand, said out loud.

Sundays: retest your error log. Twenty minutes. It's the cheapest way to win marks back that I know of.

And rest properly. Arriving in September exhausted defeats the whole point.

Do that and you arrive at the start of term already moving.

Be honest. Have you run it yet?

If yes, brilliant, skip to the last line of this email. If not, I get it, 80 minutes is a real ask on a school night, and "I'll do it at the weekend" is where good plans go to die. So here's the version that removes the excuse.

**The 25 minute version, tonight**

One question. Not a section, one long-answer question from a past paper in {$diag_worry_subject|default('your priority subject')}. Six to ten marks' worth.

Set a timer for the marks (about a minute per mark). Book closed, phone in another room. Write your answer under real conditions.

Then open the mark scheme and mark it meanly. Not "I basically said that". The scheme's exact wording earns the mark or it doesn't. For every dropped mark, one letter in your log: K if you didn't know it, R if you knew it but couldn't pull it out, W if you knew it and worded it wrong, T if timing or misreading cost you.

That's it. One question, honestly marked, letters logged. Twenty-five minutes, and you've started the evidence file that tells you exactly where your marks are leaking.

The full drill can wait for the weekend. The habit can't. Small and tonight beats perfect and never, and that's not a motivational quote, it's just how streaks start.

Run your question tonight, then reply to this email with your letters. Even if it's one W and one T. I read every reply, and I'll tell you what your pattern means.

Keep going!

Waleed

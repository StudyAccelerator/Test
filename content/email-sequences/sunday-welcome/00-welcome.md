You're in. Here's the deal you've just signed up to, because I think an inbox promise should be stated plainly.

**Every Sunday at 5pm**, one email: a single thing school never taught you about revision, exams or memory, taught fully enough to use that evening. Not a links roundup, not "5 quick tips", one real teach. Sunday evening on purpose: it's when you're staring at the week deciding what it'll look like, and the Session is built to land right inside that moment.

There's a clinic section where I answer one reader's question straight, a high-yield and low-yield pair (one thing worth your hours this week, one thing to stop doing), and occasionally a door to something of mine, always labelled honestly as exactly that.

Since you shouldn't have to wait until Sunday for the first taste, here's this week's pair now.

**High-yield:** end every revision session with ten minutes of book-closed retrieval. Whatever you studied, close everything and write what you remember. If you can't produce it three metres from your notes, you couldn't produce it in an exam hall, and it's better to find out tonight.

**Low-yield:** re-copying notes to make them neater. Neatness is formatting, not memory. The ugly page you produced from a closed book is worth ten beautiful ones copied from an open one.

One thing before Sunday, and it makes your issues genuinely better: reply and tell me your year and your subjects. One line, like "Year 13, bio chem maths". The clinic questions and examples come from what readers actually take, so that one line quietly shapes what you get.

See you Sunday at 5.

Keep going!

{$name},

Your call with me is booked, and I'm genuinely looking forward to it. These calls are the best part of my week, because they're where reports turn into actual plans.

Here's how to get the most out of your thirty minutes.

**Have two things to hand.** The diagnostic report, if you've taken the diagnostic. If you haven't, it's five minutes on the site, and it makes the call twice as useful. And the real grades: the most recent tests or mocks, actual numbers, not the summary version. I'm a doctor, I work best from the real chart.

**Know your one goal.** Calls wander when nobody names the destination. Yours might be "a plan for the next month", or "working out whether the method or the subject is the problem", or "whether any of your programmes fit, honestly". Any of those is perfect. Just pick one before we speak.

**And what I'll bring:** six years of teaching A-level students, over 1,000 of them, and a promise you can hold me to: you'll leave with a specific plan for the next few weeks, and if my programmes don't fit your situation, I'll say so without being asked. The call is free because the advice is the product I'm proudest of.

**The one thing to do now:** reply to this email with the question you most want answered when we speak. One line is enough. We'll start there, and the whole call gets better for it.

If the time stops working, no drama, just use the original booking link to move it.

Talk soon,

Waleed

{$name},

Your call with me is booked. I'm looking forward to it, and I mean that non-automatically: these calls are the best part of my week, because they're where reports turn into plans.

Here's how to squeeze the most out of your thirty minutes.

**Have two things to hand.** The diagnostic report, if you've taken the diagnostic (if you haven't, it's five minutes on the site and it turns the call from good to surgical). And the real grades: the most recent tests or mocks, actual numbers, not the summary version. I'm a doctor, I work best from the real chart.

**Know your one goal.** Calls wander when nobody names the destination. Yours might be "a plan for the next month", "whether the method or the subject is the problem", "whether any of your programmes fit, honestly". Any of those is perfect. Pick one before we speak.

**What I'll bring:** six years of teaching A-level students, over 1,000 of them, and a commitment you can hold me to: you'll leave with a specific plan for the next few weeks, and if my programmes don't fit your situation, I'll say so unprompted. The call is free because the advice is the product I'm proudest of.

**The one thing to do now:** reply to this email with the question you most want answered when we speak. One line is enough. We'll start there, and the whole call gets sharper for it.

If the time stops working, no drama, just use the original booking link to move it.

Talk soon,

Waleed

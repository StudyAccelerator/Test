**The specialists**

The Subject Accelerator sessions are taught by subject specialists, and that word has a high bar here. They got top grades in the exact subject they teach, and they've helped hundreds of students do the same.

Not someone who covers whatever's needed that week. Not a student one chapter ahead of the class. Someone who has beaten the exam your child is sitting, and knows its current shape, and has then done it again and again from the teaching side.

**Why that matters**

It matters because of what the sessions are. This isn't lecture-style teaching, where anyone with the textbook could stand at the front.

The sessions work on students' real answers against the real mark schemes, live. To coach that, you need someone who knows the scheme well enough to say "that phrase won't score, this one will" straight away. That's a specialist's skill, and it's the whole product.

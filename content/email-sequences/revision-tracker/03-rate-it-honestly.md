Hi {$name},

Your whole tracker week was built from three little words: Struggling, Shaky, Solid. The weakest topics got the most time.

Which means the plan is exactly as good as your honesty was. And this is where everyone's brain cheats a little.

**Here's how the cheating works.**

You look at a topic, you know you've seen it, and seen feels like known. So you click Solid.

But spotting a topic on a list and writing it out in an exam are two different skills. The exam only pays for the second one.

Every topic you rate too kindly gives its time away to a topic that needed it less. The plan still looks perfect on the wall, it's just aimed at slightly the wrong things.

**The 10 second honesty test.**

Ask yourself this before you rate any topic: could I write half a page on this right now, book closed, and get it mostly right?

Not "have I seen it", and not "did we do it in class". Could I produce it, now, from nothing?

If the honest answer is "probably not", that's Shaky, whatever your notes look like. And if the question makes you slightly nervous, that's Struggling, and it's just earned its Blurt and Fix.

**Nobody sees these ratings but you.**

There are no marks for a flattering dashboard. The whole value of the tracker comes from telling it the truth.

And honestly, the students who improve fastest often have the worst looking first week on paper. They were honest enough to point the hours where they were needed most.

**When Sunday comes, re-rate everything before you build next week.**

Topics you rebuilt this week move up. Anything that faded moves back down, no shame attached, because fading is just what memory does.

That way next week's plan gets built on fresh evidence instead of last week's hope.

**Today's move:** your scheduled block, plus one look back at your ratings. If one of them was too kind, knock it down now. Ten seconds, and next Sunday's plan gets sharper.

Keep going!

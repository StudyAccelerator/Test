But here's a pattern I see all the time: a student gets organised, runs the plan, feels genuinely better, and the marks still don't move. Organised hours that still leak. Because a timetable can tell you when to study and which topic, but it can't see how you study inside each block, and that's where marks are actually won or lost.

And some leak in the exam itself: they know it and still lose the marks, because knowing something and scoring with it are two completely different skills. Same timetable, three completely different problems, three different fixes.

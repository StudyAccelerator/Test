Hi {$name},

Quick one today, and it's about your parents.

If revision ever causes friction at home, here's a thing most students never work out. Your parents aren't going off your grades. They're going off what they can see.

And revision is mostly out of sight. They can't see what's in your head. What they see is the phone breaks, the door shut, and the "I did loads today" with nothing to point at.

The nagging isn't because they doubt you. It's because they can't see the work, and that has a very easy fix.

**Show them the plan.**

The printed week, the finished sessions, the stack building up if you've kept the old ones. A system they can see changes the question from "have you revised?" to "how's the plan going?". That's a much nicer question to live with.

Students tell me the nagging drops within a week of the plan going up on the wall. Evidence calms parents. It's very like medicine that way.

**Today's move:** forward this email to a parent. The section below is written for them, from me. It explains what you're doing and why it works, in their language.

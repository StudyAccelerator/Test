Quick one today, and it's about your parents.

If revision is ever a source of friction at home, here's something most students never realise: your parents aren't reacting to your grades, they're reacting to what they can see. And revision is mostly invisible. They can't see what's in your head, so they see the phone breaks, the door shut, the "I did loads today" with nothing to point at. The nagging isn't doubt about you, it's blindness about the process, and it has a surprisingly easy fix.

Show them the wall. The printed week, the finished sessions, the stack building up if you've kept the old ones. A visible system changes the conversation from "have you revised?" to "how's the plan going?", which is a much better conversation to live inside. Students tell me the nagging drops within a week of the plan going up somewhere public. Evidence calms parents. It's very like medicine that way.

So today's move: forward this email to a parent, with the section below for them. It says what you're doing and why it works, in parent language, from me.

The best thing you can do with it is ask to see the week, put it somewhere visible, and let the plan answer your questions instead of them.
